# Editorial Policy

*Effective September 4, 2026*

## What we publish

Practical, grounded content on health, fitness, nutrition, mindset, faith-aligned living, and sustainable habits. We aim for guidance that still holds up in five years, not content built for a news cycle.

## Standards

Every article, whether written by our founder, a contributor, or as part of a sponsored feature:

- Provides real education, not just positioning
- Makes claims that are evidence-based or clearly framed as personal experience
- Contains no exaggerated health promises or misleading language
- Says so plainly where the evidence is unsettled or thin
- Links out only where the link is useful to the reader
- Is written by a named human. We do not publish generic, templated, or AI-spun content.

## Who writes here

Every article carries a byline linking to that author's full profile. Paul Meisner's is at [/author/paul-meisner/](/author/paul-meisner/); guest contributors, when we run them, get the same treatment.

Contributors are invited on the strength of their expertise. Nobody pays to be a contributor and nobody is paid to write.

## Health content specifically

Our founder is not a physician and does not present as one. Where content involves clinical subject matter, we either bring in a licensed practitioner or we clearly frame the content as educational and point readers to their own provider.

We do not publish content intended to diagnose, treat, or replace medical care. See our [Disclaimer](/disclaimer/).

## Sourcing

Where we cite research, we link to the source. We prefer primary sources and peer-reviewed work over secondary coverage. We try to note when a study is small, preliminary, industry-funded, or in animals rather than humans, because those things matter and are usually omitted.

## Corrections

We get things wrong sometimes. When we do:

- Factual errors are corrected promptly
- Substantive corrections carry a dated note on the article
- Articles that are materially updated show a "last updated" date

If you spot an error, tell us: `[CONTACT EMAIL]`. Corrections are welcome and we mean that.

## Sponsored content

Paid features follow the same editorial standards as everything else and are clearly labeled. See our [Disclosure](/disclosure/) for the full picture.

## Independence

No advertiser, partner, or affiliate program has approval over our content. We've declined partnerships that didn't meet these standards and we'll do it again.
