# Cookie Policy

*Effective September 4, 2026*

## What cookies are

Small text files a site stores on your device. They let a site remember things between visits and help us understand how the site is used.

## What we use

**Strictly necessary.** Required for the site to function — security, form submission, remembering your cookie preferences. These can't be turned off.

**Analytics.** We do not currently run analytics tooling on this site. If that changes, the tool will be named here — and, if you're in the EU or UK, a consent banner will block it until you opt in.

**Functional.** Remember preferences like light or dark mode.

We do not currently use advertising or pixel-based cookies. If that changes, this page and our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) will be updated before any such cookie is set.

## Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by services we use rather than by us — form processing, embedded video players, or social embeds. Those providers have their own policies.

## Managing cookies

Use your browser settings to view and control cookies. Blocking all cookies may break parts of the site.

- Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge all let you view and delete cookies in Settings under Privacy.
