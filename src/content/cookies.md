# Cookie Policy

*Effective September 8, 2026*

## What cookies are

Small text files a site stores on your device. They let a site remember things between visits and help us understand how the site is used.

## What we use

**Strictly necessary.** Required for the site to function — security, form submission, remembering your cookie preferences. These can't be turned off.

**Analytics.** We do not currently run analytics tooling on this site. If that changes, the tool will be named here before it goes live, and this page will describe what it collects.

**Functional.** Remember preferences like light or dark mode.

## Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by services we use rather than by us — our comment system, embedded video players, and social embeds, if and when they're used on a given page. Those providers have their own policies.

## Managing cookies

Use the cookie settings on this site, or your browser settings, to control them. Blocking all cookies may break parts of the site.

Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge all let you view and delete cookies in Settings under Privacy.

## Changes

We'll update this page when what we use changes.
