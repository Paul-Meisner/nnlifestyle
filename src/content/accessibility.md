# Accessibility

*Last reviewed September 8, 2026*

We want this site usable by everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive technology.

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. That means readable contrast, descriptive alt text, proper heading structure, keyboard-navigable controls, and labeled form fields.

We're not claiming perfection. If you hit something on this site you can't use, tell us at `paul.meisner.cs@gmail.com` and describe what happened. We'll fix it and we'll tell you when it's fixed.
