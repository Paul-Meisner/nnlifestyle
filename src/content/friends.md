## Who We Feature

We work with contributors in areas such as:

- Nutrition and supplementation
- Fitness and performance
- Mental health and mindset
- Faith-aligned health and wellness
- Chiropractic and physical health
- Lifestyle practices rooted in sustainability and truth

All contributors are selected based on expertise, values, and relevance to our audience.

## Editorial Standards

We maintain clear editorial guidelines for all contributors:

- Content must provide real value and education
- Claims should be evidence-based or experience-driven
- No exaggerated health promises or misleading language
- Natural brand mentions only, no forced promotion
- Links must be relevant and helpful to the reader

We do not publish generic, templated, or AI-spun content.

## Interested in Contributing?

If you're a brand, practitioner, or professional aligned with a no-nonsense approach to health and wellness, we're open to collaboration.

📩 Reach out through our [Contact page](/about/) with:

- A brief introduction
- Your area of expertise
- Content ideas or collaboration goals
