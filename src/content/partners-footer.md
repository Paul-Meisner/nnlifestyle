## What we look for

We feature brands and practices working in:

- Nutrition and supplementation
- Fitness and performance
- Mental health and mindset
- Faith-aligned health and wellness
- Chiropractic and physical health
- Sustainable lifestyle practices

Beyond category fit, three things decide it:

**Claims that hold up.** If the marketing promises something the evidence doesn't support, that's the end of the conversation.

**A real operation.** Named people, a track record, actual customers. Not a white-label store with a logo on it.

**A long view.** Brands built to still be here in ten years, run by people who talk about their work that way.

## Editorial standards for featured content

Every feature on this site, paid or not, meets the same bar:

- Provides real value and education, not just positioning
- Claims are evidence-based or clearly framed as experience
- No exaggerated health promises or misleading language
- Brand mentions read naturally — no forced promotion
- Links are relevant and useful to the reader
- Nothing generic, templated, or AI-spun

We reserve the right to decline or pull a feature if a partner's claims change.

## Become a partner

If you're building something in health, fitness, or wellness that holds up to scrutiny, there's a place for it here.

What a feature includes:

- A dedicated feature page on Now or Never, written by Paul Meisner
- A permanent URL you can cite in your own press and marketing
- Placement in relevant editorial content where it fits naturally
- A partner badge for your own site

Pricing and formats: available on request.

To start a conversation, use the [contact form](/contact/) and select "Partnership or brand feature." Include a short introduction, what you're building, and what you're hoping to get out of it.

Every inquiry is reviewed. Not every brand is a fit, and we'll tell you plainly if it isn't.
