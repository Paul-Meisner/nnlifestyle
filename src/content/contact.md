# Contact

> Real questions get real answers.

Now or Never is a small operation, which means the person reading your message is the person who writes the site. Tell me what you're actually after and you'll hear back.

## What you can reach out about

### Partnerships and brand features

Interested in being featured, sponsoring content, or working together on a campaign? Start with the [partners page](/partners/) — it covers formats, placement types, and pricing. If what you need isn't listed there, use the form below and say so.

### Contributor and guest content

Practitioners, coaches, and professionals with something specific to say. Send a short overview of your background and the idea. One paragraph is enough.

### Expert commentary

If you have clinical, coaching, or industry expertise and are open to being quoted or consulted for articles, get in touch. I'd rather build a bench than chase sources on deadline.

### Reader questions and feedback

Corrections especially. If something on this site is wrong, I want to know.

## Before you write

One standard, applied to everything: be specific.

A templated pitch that could have been sent to four hundred other sites gets deleted. A three-sentence note that shows you've read something here and know what you're proposing gets a reply, even if the answer is no.

That's the whole filter.
