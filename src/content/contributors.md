# Contributors

Who writes here, and why we asked them.

Now or Never is written primarily by its founder. When a subject calls for expertise beyond that, we bring in someone who has it rather than researching our way to a confident-sounding article.

Guest contributors are invited on the strength of what they know. We don't pay for guest articles and nobody pays us to publish one. Where a contributor is connected to a brand featured on this site, we say so on their entry.
