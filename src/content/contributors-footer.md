## How guest contributors are selected

Three things:

**They know something specific.** Not general wellness enthusiasm — an actual domain where they've done the work and can say something a well-read generalist couldn't.

**They'll say what they don't know.** The most useful contributors are the ones who mark the edges of the evidence instead of writing past them.

**They fit how this site thinks.** Long view, no hype, comfortable saying the boring answer is usually the right one.

## Want to write for us?

We're open to it, selectively.

Send a short note through the [contact form](/contact/) with "Contributor or guest content" selected. Include who you are, what you'd want to write about, and why you're the person to write it. One or two paragraphs.

What we don't accept: pre-written drafts pitched cold, articles whose real purpose is a link, and anything generic enough that it could have been sent to a hundred other sites. Guest articles are not for sale in either direction. If you're looking for paid placement, that's the [partners page](/partners/) — a different thing, clearly labeled.
